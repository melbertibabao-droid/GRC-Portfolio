## 🎯 Focus Areas

My work in this repository demonstrates practical application across the six NIST CSF 2.0 core functions:

* **Govern (GV):** Establishing organizational cybersecurity risk management strategy, expectations, and policy.
* **Identify (ID):** Asset management, risk assessment, and identifying vulnerabilities.
* **Protect (PR):** Implementing safeguards to ensure delivery of critical services.
* **Detect (DE):** Identifying the occurrence of cybersecurity events.
* **Respond (RS):** Taking action regarding a detected cybersecurity incident.
* **Recover (RC):** Restoring capabilities or services impaired due to an incident.
